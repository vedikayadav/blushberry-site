Good to know

Do they need water or care?+

No water and no sunlight. Keep them dry and dust them now and then with a soft brush or a hair dryer on the cool setting.

How long do they last?+

Kept away from damp and strong direct sun, they hold their shape and colour for years.

Can I choose my own colours?+

Yes. Send a photo, an outfit or a palette and we will match the stems as closely as our chenille shades allow.

How far ahead should I order?+

Single stems and decor pieces take about 1 day to make and bouquets about 2 days, plus shipping time. Festive weeks fill up first, so order early for Diwali and Valentine's.

Do you deliver?+

Yes, we ship anywhere in Canada for a flat $9.99. Once your payment is processed, we email you a confirmation with your estimated delivery date.

Is the tutorial guide a physical book?+

No. It is a printable PDF you download right after checkout and print at home. Digital guides have no shipping cost.
